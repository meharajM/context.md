# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who capture thoughts from conversations, work, and daily life, then need them retained and organized into useful local context without switching between many task tools.

## Product Purpose

Thought Notes turns typed thoughts, shared text, pasted text, and user-started voice captures into durable local topic context on Android and iOS. It saves the original before on-device synthesis, files clear notes into topics, keeps uncertain or failed captures in Inbox, and makes saved notes searchable and portable through user-controlled backups.

## Brand Personality

Calm, private, practical. The interface should reduce friction and make data handling understandable without overselling AI.

## Anti-references

- Cluttered, generic AI dashboards and repeated stacks of promotional cards.
- Always-listening capture; recording starts only after an explicit user action. Keep Android's independent widget capture and iOS's app-opening widget affordance easy to reach.
- Decorative AI effects, urgency, or unexplained processing states.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve the user's original thought before any optional model work.
2. Keep capture primary and show advanced organization controls when needed.
3. Make local processing, model availability, and Inbox recovery easy to understand.
4. Use familiar mobile controls and consistent visual hierarchy.
5. Prefer compact, quiet surfaces over repeated containers and decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use screen-reader labels and actions, support scalable text and readable contrast, keep touch targets comfortably sized, respect reduced-motion preferences, and retain usable keyboard interaction where available.
